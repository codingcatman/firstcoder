A single, considered object — where structure becomes surface, and surface becomes light. The Dome is an exercise in restraint: everything necessary, nothing more.

01 — Concept

An idea, distilled

The Dome began as a question: how little can a form say, and still say everything? Its curved shell is not decoration but the whole argument — a continuous surface that shelters, directs, and holds.

Rotate it. Every angle is intentional. There is no back, no front, no seam to hide — only one uninterrupted gesture from base to apex.

02 — Form

Form that follows physics

The geometry is derived, not styled. The profile you see is the shape a load wants to take — a dome distributes force along its own curvature, so the material can be thin and the presence can be quiet.

Zoom in and the proportion holds at every scale: the same clean arc whether it sits in your hand or across a room.

03 — Craft

Made to be kept

Modelled from a single CAD surface and built to close tolerances, The Dome is engineered for parts that meet without a gap. What looks effortless is the result of many quiet decisions.

It is designed to age well — to be the kind of object you stop noticing, and then would not part with.
